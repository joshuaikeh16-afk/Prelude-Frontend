Privacy Policy

Rixie helps you prepare for events by organizing schedules, tasks, goals, reminders, and related information. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect account details such as your email address, nickname, and profile picture, along with events, tasks, schedules, goals, reminders, and images you choose to provide.

If you sign in with Google, we use the account information needed to authenticate your account.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to operate your account, provide planning tools, deliver reminders, maintain security, troubleshoot issues, and improve the service.

3. Sign-in Providers

If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides the account information needed to authenticate you. You can manage third-party account access through your Google Account security settings.

4. Storage and Sharing

We use service providers to operate Rixie, which may include database, hosting, authentication, and email delivery providers. Information may be processed by these providers as needed to deliver the service. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect users and the service.

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of online storage or transmission is completely secure.

5. Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide Rixie and meet legal or security obligations. You may request account and personal data deletion by contacting us. Some information may be retained where the law requires it.

6. Your Choices

You can choose whether to connect optional integrations and can revoke Google permissions. You may contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law.

7. Children and Changes

Rixie is not intended for children below the minimum age permitted under applicable law. We may update this policy and will publish the revised version on this page.
